# Post from Call

Your best content is already in your calls: real objections, real numbers, real stories. This skill turns one call into 3 posts that sound like you, without exposing the client.

## Inputs
- Call notes, a transcript, or a voice dump. Messy is fine. Voice dictation tools like Wispr Flow are great for this: talk for 3 minutes right after the call, paste the text.
- `voice.md` in the current folder (optional but strongly recommended). If missing, ask for 3 posts the user likes that they wrote, or write in plain, direct, short-sentence style and suggest creating voice.md from the template.
- `icp.md` (optional) so posts speak to the buyer, not to peers.

## Steps
1. **Extract.** From the notes, list:
   - The buyer's problem in their words (quotes are gold).
   - The moment of insight: what surprised them or you.
   - Any number: time, money, count, before and after.
   - The objection and how it was answered.
   - One thing you would tell every prospect like this.
   Show this list to the user in 5 to 8 bullets and ask: "Anything here off-limits?"
2. **Anonymise.** Replace names, companies and unique details with industry plus geography ("a US fractional CFO", "a 40-person Australian recruitment agency"). Round numbers if they could identify the client. Never publish a client name without written permission.
3. **Write 3 posts, 3 different shapes:**
   - **Post 1, Story**: what happened on the call, the turn, the lesson. 120 to 220 words.
   - **Post 2, Opinion**: one clear point of view that came out of the call, with the reason and a short example. 80 to 160 words.
   - **Post 3, How-to or list**: the practical steps or checklist, numbered. 120 to 250 words.
4. **Hook.** First line under 12 words, specific, no clickbait. Write 3 hook options for each post and pick one.
5. **Ending.** One soft call to action or question. Match the CTA style in voice.md (for example "Comment KIT and I'll send it").
6. **Check voice.** Compare against voice.md: words they use, words they never use, line breaks, emoji rules. Fix anything off.

## Output format

```
### Raw material
- Problem (their words): "..."
- Insight: ...
- Number: ...
- Objection + answer: ...

### Post 1: Story (168 words)
Hook options:
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...
Chosen: 2

<post text>

### Post 2: Opinion (112 words)
...

### Post 3: How-to (204 words)
...

Anonymised: <what you changed>
```

## Rules
- Sound like the user, not like a brand. Use "I" when it was the user.
- One idea per post. Short lines, white space, no walls of text.
- Real details only. Never invent a quote or a number that was not in the notes.
- No client names, no screenshots of private messages, no details that identify a person.
- No em dashes. No hype words: unlock, game-changer, revolutionize, leverage, seamless, cutting-edge, supercharge, delve.
- Hashtags: none unless voice.md says otherwise.

## Example
Voice dump: "Call with a recruitment agency owner in Sydney, 40 people. They sent 2,000 connection requests last quarter, got 6 calls. Openers were all about them, 'we are a leading agency'. I showed them 3 openers about the client's open roles. She said 'so we've been talking to ourselves for 3 months'."

Post 1 hook: "They sent 2,000 invites and booked 6 calls."
Post 2 hook: "Your opener is not about you. Ever."
Post 3 hook: "3 openers that start with their open roles, not your agency:"
